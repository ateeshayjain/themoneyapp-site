# The Money App — Support

The Money App is a privacy-first personal finance tracker for India.
Your data stays on your device: no servers, no accounts, no tracking.

## Get help

- **Email:** [Ateeshay.jain@gmail.com](mailto:Ateeshay.jain@gmail.com)
  — include your iOS version and a description of the issue. The app's
  **Report a Problem** screen (Profile → About & Legal) attaches
  diagnostics only if you choose to share them.
- We aim to respond within 2 business days.

## Common questions

- **Where is my data?** On your iPhone, protected by iOS data
  protection. Use Profile → Data → Backup & Restore for encrypted
  backups you control.
- **What does the AI see?** AI features are bring-your-own-key: content
  you send goes directly from your device to Anthropic under your own
  API key. Details in the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
